Hi, I’m Vasti Tait, the creator of [Momster Monologues](https://momster-monologues.github.io/). I started this blog in 2025 as a way to share my journey, my passions, and my mission to foster expression and empowerment for everyone.

As an advocate for [self-acceptance](queen-within), I believe that everyone deserves to be heard and celebrated for who they truly are. Through art and drama therapy, I strive to help people find their voices, confront their fears, and embrace their unique truths.

> "The most courageous act is still to think for yourself. Aloud." – Coco Chanel

As a proud [mother of many](takes-a-village), I dedicate myself to nurturing a generation of resilient, confident individuals who are unafraid to embrace their individuality and live fully. My approach is grounded in joy, self-love, and the belief that life's challenges can be transformed into moments of growth and self-discovery.

I'm also incredibly fortunate to share my life with my amazing husband, my [partner in crime](https://momster-monologues.github.io/better-together). Together, we believe that communication, transparency, and support are the pillars of a strong relationship. Life is about having fun and growing together, and I’m blessed to walk this path by his side.

Through this blog, I hope to connect with and inspire others to find their strength, their voice, and the joy that comes from embracing who they are.
